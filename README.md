## How I work

I design for the questions a reviewer will ask later:

- Where did this data come from?
- What changed, who changed it, and when?
- Can the decision be reproduced and explained?
- Are tests, documentation, and deployment evidence aligned?

That means versioned requirements, traceability, explicit validation boundaries, automated checks, and clear documentation—not just a working demo.
